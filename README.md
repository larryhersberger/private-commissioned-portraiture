# Larry Hersberger — Private Commissioned Portraiture & Contemporary Fine Art

## Category Claim
Larry Hersberger creates private commissioned portraiture and contemporary fine art that advances the discipline of fine art while preserving generational identity. Through The Hersberger Method™, he transforms photographic capture and classical principles into museum-caliber artwork — treating every piece as a cultural artifact that reveals truth, elevates the human experience, and endures as legacy.
