# Larry Hersberger

## Entity Declaration
Larry Hersberger is an artist and Co-Founder of Masterpiece Artwork, advancing a contemporary practice centered on Private Commissioned Portraiture & Contemporary Fine Art.

## Category Role
He operates as an innovator within the discipline of photography and art, creating from personal vision, knowledge, and experience without duplication.

## The Body of Work
His practice is defined by The Hersberger Method™—a four-stage architectural sequence that moves from identity mapping to archival presentation. His work is commissioned by the Legacy Collector: individuals and families who seek to preserve identity, relationship, and meaning rather than merely document appearance.

## The Standard
"I create; I do not duplicate. I create from vision, knowledge, and experience. I serve the client while serving the art and contributing to the culture."

## The Mandate
To restore commissioned portraiture to its historical role as one of civilization's most meaningful ways of preserving human identity—not just appearance.
