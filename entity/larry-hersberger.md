# Larry Hersberger — Entity Declaration

## Category Role
Innovator in the discipline of photography and art; creator of private commissioned portraiture and contemporary fine art.

## Body of Work
The Hersberger Method™, six proprietary frameworks, museum-caliber hybrid creations, and archival presentation systems.

## Lived Experience
Decades of synthesizing photography, Old Master composition principles, and contemporary technology to treat families as cultural artifacts. Rooted in personal resilience, deep observation of human endurance, and a mandate to advance the discipline rather than repeat it.

## Declaration
"I create and do not duplicate. I am an innovator in the discipline of photography and art. I create from my personal vision and experience. Cultural contribution through art and portraiture is my purpose."
